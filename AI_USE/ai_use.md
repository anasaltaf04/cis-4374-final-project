## Prompt engineering takeaways

1. **Give the actual requirements.** My HW4–6 summary produced work that missed most requirements; reading out the real requirements fixed it.
2. **Assign a role.** "Act as a PM / product owner / business analyst" produces professional artifacts without asking for each element.
3. **Supply context.** Naming existing IDs, dates, and sections keeps new work consistent with old work.
4. **Use numbers as constraints.** "At least four risks per category" and "one Accountable per activity" make output checkable.
5. **Name the format and tool.** "Excel with formulas" gave a model that recalculates; "one item per line" pasted straight into Trello.
6. **State acceptance criteria and stop conditions.** "Keep figures consistent" and "stop if it depends on the board" act like a Definition of Done.
7. **Brief first, build once.** Separating "here are the requirements, don't start" from "now build" produced one consistent pass.
8. **Ask for sources on factual work.** The postmortem prompt required citable facts, which made verification possible.
